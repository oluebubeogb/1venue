Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026. This page is for 1venue at 1venue.live.

Who we are

1venue is a live events and meetings platform. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. The operator is identified here as “1venue”. If you have questions or want to exercise your rights, email legal@1venue.live.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account. When you create an account we store your email address, name, username, phone number, and a password that is hashed with bcrypt. We never store your password in plain text. Your username identifies your account on 1venue. Your phone number is stored only as contact information for your account; we do not share it with other people, we do not use it for marketing, and we do not verify it by SMS today. Google accounts get a username automatically (you can change it in Settings) and may add a phone number later.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose “Continue with Google”, we receive your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier through OpenID Connect (openid, email, and profile scopes). We do not request or receive any other Google user data (for example Gmail, Drive, or contacts). 1venue’s use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Cookies and session data. We use essential, HttpOnly session cookies (a short-lived access token and a refresh token) so we can keep you signed in securely. They are host-only, use SameSite=Lax, and are marked Secure on HTTPS. During Google sign-in we also set a short-lived HttpOnly cookie to complete the login. We keep a record of your sessions (device/browser, IP address, sign-in method, and last activity) so you can review and sign out devices. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party advertising pixels.
  • Profile and uploads. You may add a display name, avatar, and other profile details. Files you upload (avatars, event covers, chat attachments, materials, whiteboard exports, and similar) are stored in our file storage — on this deployment, Cloudflare R2.
  • Meeting and event content. Content you and others create in a room or event may be stored so the event can run and so hosts can review it later. This can include chat, polls, Q&A, comments, reactions, whiteboard data, attendance records, event and venue details, and uploaded files.
  • Guests. You can join a room as a guest with just a display name. That name is shown to others in the room. We do not require an account for guest access when the host allows it.
  • Audio and video. Live audio, video, and screen share are sent through our self-hosted LiveKit media server so the meeting can take place. We do not keep a server-side recording of the live media. If a host records a session, the recording is made on that person’s own device and is saved there; it is not uploaded to 1venue. We may keep metadata that a recording was started or stopped (who and when).
  • Captions and transcripts. When live captions are turned on, short audio clips may be sent to a configured speech-to-text provider so speech can be turned into text. Caption text is shown in the room and, when transcripts are enabled, stored as part of the session history.
  • Text-to-speech. When a read-out feature is used, the text to be spoken is sent to Replicate to generate audio. Generated audio may be cached in our file storage so the same text is not generated twice.
  • AI features. Optional AI features (for example summaries) send the relevant text to the AI provider configured for this server.
  • Email. We send transactional email (for example to confirm your address, reset a password, or welcome you) through our configured SMTP email provider.

How we use information

We use this information to provide, secure, and improve 1venue: to create and sign you into your account, host and join events, deliver live media, show captions and optional AI or read-out features, store the content you choose to share, send essential email, prevent abuse, and comply with the law. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 applies, we process personal data on the bases it allows, including that the processing is necessary to provide the service you asked for, that we have a legitimate interest in running and securing the platform, or that you have given consent (for example when you sign in with Google). Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, you also have the rights described there.

In general you may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. To make a request, email legal@1venue.live. We may need to confirm it is you before we act.

How long we keep information

We keep account information and the content attached to your account until you ask us to delete it or the account is closed. Session cookies and session records last until they expire or you sign out (including “sign out everywhere”). Live room state is temporary. Transcripts, session history, attendance, and similar event records are kept so hosts can review past sessions, and we delete them when you or the host asks us to, or when we no longer need them. We may retain limited information if the law requires it (for example to resolve a dispute or prevent abuse).

Who we share information with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services that help us operate 1venue, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Contabo (Germany/EU), which hosts the 1venue application and our LiveKit media server.
  • Cloudflare, for DNS, content delivery, and R2 file storage.
  • Google, only if you sign in with Google, and only to complete that sign-in.
  • Our SMTP email provider, to send the emails described above.
  • A speech-to-text provider, when captions are enabled.
  • Replicate, when text-to-speech is used.
  • The AI provider configured for this server, when you use an AI feature.

Some of these providers may process data outside Nigeria. We use them only as needed to run the service. Other people in a room or event can see the content you share there (your name, chat, video, and so on), as you would expect in a live meeting.

Children

1venue is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or given us information, please email legal@1venue.live and we will delete it.

Security

We hash passwords, use HttpOnly session cookies, serve the site over HTTPS, and restrict access to account and meeting data. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. If a change is material, we will provide a more prominent notice, for example on the site or by email.

Contact

Email legal@1venue.live. This is the address to use for privacy questions, deletion or access requests, and any notice under this policy.
